# AYANA conditional-GO pilot — CEO review design

**17 October 2026 · Plan only. No publication, contact, production experiment write, or paid activation authorized.**

This revises [the acquisition-agent architecture](CEO_ACQUISITION_AGENT_PLAN.md). One lightweight orchestrator would compare permitted actions, request approval for one exact action, guide its execution, and measure the result. This document selects a candidate experiment for review; it does **not** approve execution.

## Evidence and limits

The independent audit found **0 actionable Hacker News opportunities** and no reproducible production Precision@5. A public [NRI discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/nri/comments/1uwnjxf/parent_living_alone_in_india_tips_to_make_life/) describes an adult child in the US seeking practical ways to support a mother living alone in India. A [caregiver medication discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/caregivers/comments/1iaye25/struggling_to_manage_my_parents/) asks for better reminder systems, but is historical. These are evidence of *problem language*, not evidence that either author wants AYANA or consents to outreach. AYANA's own site could not be inspected from this environment, so current page, CTA, audience, analytics, and product claims require founder verification.

The three proposals below are **acquisition experiments**, not three purported customer leads. Estimated reach is deliberately ordinal because no AYANA channel baseline is available. No competitor or channel advantage is claimed from these examples.

**Provisional ranking rule:** First reject any action without channel ownership/permission, truthful claims, approval path, and measurable outcome. Among remaining actions, prefer the stronger match between documented need and accessible audience, then lower founder time and cost. Reach estimates do not pretend to be forecasts. LinkedIn or partnership could outrank search after the founder supplies actual audience or partner data; that would be a documented rerank, not quiet score manipulation.

### Recommendation: experiment 1, AYANA-owned practical page

It uses the most direct documented problem, is under AYANA's control, requires no unsolicited contact, and can create a durable resource for later search, AI-search, or approved owned-channel distribution. The 28-day test may show poor search reach; that is a valid outcome. **Alternative 2 is rejected for this first run** because AYANA's NRI follower reach is unknown and a broad social impression is weak evidence of a qualified customer. **Alternative 3 is rejected** because no opted-in partner is established and founder effort is higher. **A Reddit reply is excluded**, not ranked: [Reddit requires a separate agreement for commercial API use](https://redditinc.com/policies/data-api-terms), and a public request is not outreach permission.

## CEO approval boundary

No page is published, post sent, partner approached, or other external action taken from this plan. After the founder confirms site access and measurement, the orchestrator must present the **exact page copy, verified product claims, URL/CTA, expected cost, publication channel, and 28-day measurement sheet**. The CEO must explicitly approve that concrete version. A change to copy, destination, distribution channel, or paid cost requires renewed approval. No automated spam, unsolicited messaging, or automatic posting is in scope.

## Manual baseline, then assisted recommendation

Before showing FounderReach's ranked output, give the AYANA founder the same one-page product brief, budget, three channel options, and 60-minute timebox. Ask them to independently find demand evidence, choose one experiment, prepare its outline and CTA, identify permission risks, and specify how they would measure it. Record actual active minutes, total elapsed time, source links and dates, candidate actions considered, selected action, copy preparation time, and mistakes or unsupported claims. Preserve the unedited baseline artifact.

Then use the existing FounderReach code plus a **manual orchestration worksheet** to perform the same task. Record founder active time separately from analyst/computer time. Two reviewers who have not seen which proposal is which score both outputs against a frozen rubric: target-customer fit, source evidence, permission, claim accuracy, action feasibility, and measurement quality (0–2 each). Record whether the founder accepts either recommendation and why. The founder sees the manual task first, so learning may carry into the assisted task; this single-founder comparison is **directional, not causal proof of superiority**. Do not claim time saved unless both clocks and preparation quality support it.

## One 28-day measurement protocol

**Day 0:** Freeze the chosen action, founder approval, source evidence, baseline, page URL, and attribution plan. Record pre-publication traffic to the relevant AYANA pages and current inquiry rate if available. Founder publishes manually after approval. If no site analytics, CTA event, inquiry capture, or Search Console access exists, mark the test **not ready** rather than inventing measurements.

**Days 1–28:** Record the unique page's attributable visits by referrer and campaign tag, CTA clicks, and distinct inbound inquiries. Review each inquiry against this predeclared definition: the person voluntarily contacts AYANA, is an adult child/caregiver with a parent in India, and describes a check-in, medicine reminder, or living-alone safety need AYANA actually addresses. Exclude vendors, founders, generic praise, and duplicates. Record founder active minutes and any real vendor/account charges from billing records. Use [Search Console page impressions/clicks](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/searchanalytics/query) as supporting reach evidence, not as leads. AI-search exposure may be inspected through Search Console where available; no separate “GEO” ranking claim.

**Day 28:** Report visits, CTA clicks, qualified inquiries, visit→CTA and CTA→qualified-inquiry rates with denominators, founder and analyst time, actual ₹ spend, cost per qualified inquiry (N/A if none), and the manual-vs-FounderReach rubric. Also report zeroes and missing data. One attributable qualified inquiry is a **feasibility signal**, not proof of product-market fit or FounderReach superiority. If reach is minimal, diagnose distribution; if meaningful visits occur but no qualified inquiries, examine message, CTA, or offer. Predeclare the next recommendation and its evidence.

## Acceptance criteria and stop gates

1. **Decision quality:** three distinct candidate channels with source links, dated evidence, permission, reach uncertainty, effort, cost, and measurement path; one selected with explicit rejection reasons. No inferred purchase intent.
2. **Security gate:** before storing a new production experiment record, verify the **live** Supabase schema and RLS/grants, test anonymous access is denied, confirm backend key stays server-side, and reconcile cloud/SQLite IDs and key fields. A read-only check on 17 October 2026 failed with DNS `ConnectError` for both privileged and anon clients, so this gate is **open**. Existing best-effort dual-write is not a synchronization guarantee. Local paper/CSV planning may continue, but no new production record is written until the gate passes.
3. **Action gate:** exact CEO approval is recorded before any publication, contact, or external action. No automated posts or unsolicited messages.
4. **Baseline gate:** an unedited manual baseline, stopwatch data, and blind rubric exist before the assisted recommendation is reviewed.
5. **Measurement gate:** 28 full days, attributable visits, CTA clicks, qualified inquiries, founder time, and reconciled actual costs. Missing measurement is labeled missing, never treated as zero.
6. **Budget gate:** **₹0 planned paid spend**. The ₹500–₹600 operating target is available only for a later explicitly approved expense; ₹1,000 is the absolute ceiling. No paid service, ad, placement, or key activation is authorized by this plan. Vendor billing is checked independently of the app ledger.
7. **Claim gate:** declare FounderReach better than the manual baseline only if the blinded quality score is higher **and** founder active time is lower for comparable completed preparation; a single pilot still needs replication. If not, report parity or underperformance.

## Smallest implementation plan after CEO review

1. **Preflight, no new product code:** confirm AYANA site/CTA/analytics and available LinkedIn or partner data; resolve live Supabase security and cloud/local reconciliation; freeze product claims and the manual baseline form.
2. **Thin orchestration:** wrap existing profile, discovery, verifier, scorer, and budget checks in one explicit state machine that produces three `ExperimentProposal` records with evidence and policy decisions. Keep one run ID and an append-only audit trail in verified Supabase. Do not add a dashboard or multi-agent runtime.
3. **Approval and outcome:** store the exact proposed action/version and CEO approval; guide manual publication. Record visits, CTA clicks, qualified inquiries, time, and actual costs in a minimal outcome record. Never infer approval from a buttonless status or automate posting.
4. **Evaluation:** compare against the frozen manual baseline and generate a 28-day report with a next-action recommendation. Keep the existing labeled intent suite; add meaningful tests for permission, approval, budget, idempotency, and missing-data behavior when implementation starts.

No implementation step above begins on the basis of this design alone. The next CEO review should confirm the selected channel's ownership and telemetry, the baseline protocol, and the exact approval path.
