# FounderReach Founding Pilot — internal draft

**Status:** Prepared for CEO review. No prospects contacted, payment requested, or offer published. Price is a hypothesis.

## Concise offer

For three qualified founders, run a 14-day guided customer acquisition experiment. We confirm the business profile together, review credible demand evidence, choose one low-cost action the founder approves, support manual execution, and report attributable inquiries and effort. Proposed one-time price: **₹4,999**. In interviews, test willingness to pay from **₹2,000 to ₹5,000** without presenting a discount as proven pricing. We do not promise customers or rankings.

## Founder interview script (20 minutes)

1. What do you sell, to whom, and where? What would count as a qualified inquiry?
2. How do customers find you now? Which channels have produced customers in the last 30 days?
3. What have you spent on tools, agencies, ads, or your own time? What worked and failed?
4. Which customer acquisition decision is hardest to make this week?
5. If we found one credible action and helped you run and measure it for 14 days, what would make it valuable?
6. At ₹2,000, ₹3,500, and ₹5,000 one-time, which prices would you seriously consider? Why?
7. Would you pay ₹4,999 now for the described pilot? Record the answer separately from any actual payment.
8. What time can you commit, and what channels or communities can you legitimately use?
9. May we follow up with a written draft? Do not request payment before CEO approval.

## Qualification rubric

Score each item 0, 1, or 2. **Fit:** clear product and customer; **stage:** seeking first or repeatable customers; **access:** owns a website or channel; **time:** can spend at least 2 hours over 14 days; **measurement:** can identify inquiries and consent to a simple log; **permission:** willing to follow source and community rules. Maximum 12. Invite to pilot review at 9–12, clarify at 6–8, defer below 6. Do not substitute this score for willingness to pay.

## Manual execution checklist

- [ ] Record business type, current methods, spending, problems, and qualification score.
- [ ] Confirm the founder-edited profile; store confirmation date and version.
- [ ] Gather demand evidence; retain links, excerpts, timestamps and verification status.
- [ ] Rank one primary owned or permission-cleared action and two alternatives; note uncertainty.
- [ ] Establish baseline inquiries, traffic, and founder time where available. Leave unknowns blank.
- [ ] Obtain explicit founder approval of exact action, destination, and any cost.
- [ ] Execute manually only after applicable source permission and community rule checks.
- [ ] Record action time, delivery hours, direct costs, visits, inquiries and verified customers on days 0, 7 and 14.
- [ ] Report what happened, attribution limits, satisfaction and willingness to continue.
- [ ] Seek separate CEO approval before outreach, payment, publishing, or any external action.
