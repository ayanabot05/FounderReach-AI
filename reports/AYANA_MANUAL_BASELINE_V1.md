**Freeze date:** 17 October 2026. Complete this worksheet before showing the founder any new assisted recommendation. The previously published pilot proposal may bias this exercise; record whether the founder read it. Do not backfill timings.

## Identical task brief for both arms

Product: AYANA. Intended users: adult children, including NRIs, supporting parents living independently in India. Candidate channels: AYANA-owned website, an AYANA-owned social account, and an opted-in diaspora or caregiver partner. Budget: no spending without CEO approval. No automated or unsolicited messages. Choose one 28-day acquisition experiment that can produce attributable qualified inquiries. Use only truthful, founder-confirmed product claims.

## Blind-review rubric — frozen v1

Two reviewers receive redacted outputs in random A/B order, without author or tool identity. Score each dimension **0, 1, or 2**. Record a short justification and the source URL for each evidence-dependent score. Resolve disagreement by retaining both scores, not silently averaging away a dispute.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer/problem fit | Topic or persona mismatch | Plausible but inferred | Direct evidence for AYANA's target problem |
| Evidence quality | Missing or unverifiable | Linked but context/date incomplete | Linked, contextual, dated, and accurately represented |
| Permission and approval | Prohibited or unsolicited action | Rules/ownership unclear | Permitted owned/opted-in action with explicit approval path |
| Claim accuracy | Fabricated or unsupported claims | Some claims need confirmation | All product and outcome claims founder-confirmed |
| Feasibility | No executable next step | Feasible after major unknowns | Exact action, owner, timing, and dependencies specified |
| Measurement | No attributable outcome | Partial traffic or engagement metrics | Visits, CTA, qualified inquiries, time, and actual costs defined |

**Total:** 0–12. A zero on permission or claim accuracy is a safety failure regardless of total. Compare the selected actions, quality score, founder active time, analyst/machine time, and founder acceptance. Superiority is reported only if the assisted result is higher quality and reduces founder active time in a comparable completed task; one sequential trial remains directional evidence.
